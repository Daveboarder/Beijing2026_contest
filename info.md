The purpose of this activity is to provide a common dataset and evaluation protocol for comparing data-processing and classification methods in laser-induced breakdown spectroscopy.



The contest focuses on a **steel aging-state classification problem**. The goal is to **predict the aging level of a steel sample** from its LIBS spectra. The spectral data provided for this contest are raw signals directly obtained from the spectrometer.



The dataset contains 180 anonymized samples representing five aging grades:



120 labeled training samples;

60 test samples for which participants must predict the associated aging levels;

12282 wavelength values for each spectrum;

200 spectra for each sample.

The dataset was acquired under measurement conditions designed to reflect practical constraints. Spectra were collected in ambient air using a three-channel fiber spectrometer equipped with CMOS detectors, with remote measurements performed at a stand-off distance of 0.5 m. The contest therefore evaluates classification performance in the presence of measurement variability relevant to field-oriented LIBS applications.



Contest rules:



\- The classification accuracy on the test set will be used as the primary figure of merit for ranking submitted results.

\- Participants should submit a CSV file containing the predicted aging level for each test sample. The required file format is provided in sample\_submission/predictions.csv.

\- Submission files should be sent by email to libs2026@hjsmeeting.cn.

\- The submission deadline is October 31, 2026.

\- Only registered LIBS 2026 conference attendees may participate in the contest.

\- Each participant or team may submit only one final CSV file before the deadline.

\- There are no restrictions on the software tools or classification methodologies used.

\- The top-performing teams will be acknowledged in a special session at LIBS 2026 in Guangzhou and will be invited to present their methods and results.

\- The top-performing teams will also be invited to contribute, together with the contest organizers, to a manuscript for a special issue of Spectrochimica Acta Part B
